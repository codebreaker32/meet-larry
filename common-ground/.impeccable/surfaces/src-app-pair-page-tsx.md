# Larry app surfaces (pair session, card, create, join, steps, landing)

Scope: all v1 app screens. Mode: operate (landing: persuade, but inside the same world).
Audience/job: two people together on one phone find common ground, talk, save a First Step. Easy-read, tap-only possible, ≥48px targets.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md principles; The Earned Outside Rule (DESIGN.md): Glory, titles, stages and counts only for verified real-world things; never rarity, loot, random rewards or a currency, and nothing earned in chat.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your taste is a trading card. Scanning someone's card is a trade, and common ground is dealt between two cards. Refuses the default chatbot shell (white bubbles + one blue accent) and the cream-serif wellness look.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink #14121f card faces with thick domain-coloured frames (film coral #ff5a5f, TV yellow #ffd23f, music teal #2ec4b6, games violet #7b61ff, books orange #ff9f1c) on a lilac-white #f4f1ff table. Qloo poster art fills the card windows. Display type: Anybody (heavy, slightly expanded). Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible. Holo foil appears only on the card frame edge. Icons: Lucide.

STORY: I make my card by tapping pictures, show the QR code, we see what we both love and why, we chat with Larry, and we save one real plan.

FIRST VIEWPORT (pair): top bar with both mini-cards and names. A centre stage with the two taste cards tilted toward each other and the bridge cards dealt between them, each with a "because Sam likes X · Priya likes Y" line. The chat thread below, with the composer pinned at the bottom (name toggle, quick replies, mic, send). The primary action is the composer, and "End & save a First Step" sits in the top bar.

FORM: Trading Card, the model pick (my ordered list #1); seed key 8c053a11. Signature interaction: the deal, where bridge cards fly out from between the two cards once, then rest. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, a single orchestrated deal, foil sheen follows the pointer, everything off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
